          Pentatonic scales

          A pentatonic scale has only five notes per octave, which is why it is the scale most guitarists learn first for solos. Pick a key below to see the five boxes, then follow the order the guides recommend.

          The coloured dot is the root. Dots on the left or right follow your hand switch. A "0" above the nut means that string is played open. Each box has exactly two notes on every string.

          How to learn it

          Everything here comes from the guides listed at the bottom. The name in brackets is where each tip is from.

          1. Learn Box 1 first, then Box 2. "Pattern 1 comes first" and Pattern 2 "should be the second shape you learn". JustinGuitar
          2. Go slowly. Play a pattern perfectly at a very slow speed, about 10 times, before speeding up, so you don't build habits you'll have to fix later. JustinGuitar
          3. Use your fingertips. Don't let your fingers fold, and don't barre. JustinGuitar
          4. Learn where the root is. Learn the root notes on the 6th and 5th strings, and practise one shape with its root on the 6th string and one with its root on the 5th. Yousician
          5. Train your ear on the root. Start on the lowest root, play up as far as you can, come back down as low as you can, then return to the root. JustinGuitar
          6. One shape at a time. Get a shape under your fingers until you don't have to look at the chart. Then learn the shape before or after it to see how the notes link up. JG Music Lessons
          7. Make music with one shape before you master all five. JustinGuitar
          8. Don't get stuck in one box. Learn how the positions connect, then make phrases that move between them. Practise with a metronome or a backing track. Guitar River
          9. If Box 1 feels like a stretch, that is normal for beginners. You can use your ring finger instead of your little finger on the top notes of the first and second strings, especially higher up the neck. Guitar River, JustinGuitar
          10. Spot the repeats. The notes on the first and sixth strings are always the same in every box. JG Music Lessons

          Major, minor and the blues note
          
    
            
      
          • The major pentatonic is 1, 2, 3, 5, 6. The minor pentatonic is 1, b3, 4, 5, b7. Music Gurus, Yamaha
            • 
      
          • C major pentatonic and A minor pentatonic have exactly the same notes. Only the root is different, so the two sound different. Music Gurus
            • 
      
          • Because of that, the major pentatonic uses the same shapes as the minor one. Major Box 1 is the same shape as minor Box 2, major Box 2 is minor Box 3, and so on. JustinGuitar forum
            • 
      
          • Try playing a few notes of G minor pentatonic and then switching to G major pentatonic. Listen to how the root changes the sound. Yousician, Music Gurus
            • 
      
          • The blues scale is the minor pentatonic with one added note, the flat 5. Yamaha
            •

          Spider exercise
          
        
          Put your fingers 1, 2, 3 and 4 on four frets in a row, one finger per fret. Play one note with each finger in order, then move to the next string, all the way across and back. It builds finger independence, which is what makes chord changes (especially to C) feel easier.

          How to learn a pattern
      
            
        
          1. Count it out loud, "1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and", with no guitar.
            2. 
        
          2. Swing your hand down and up the whole time, even on the beats you don't strum. Just miss the strings.
            3. 
        
          3. Mute the strings with your fretting hand and practise the rhythm on one chord, at 50 to 60 BPM.
            4. 
        
          4. Only add the chord changes once the pattern runs without thinking. Then raise the speed 5 BPM at a time.

          Tuning
          
    
          Tap a string to hear its note, then tune yours until it matches. Always start with the string a bit flat and tighten up to the note.
